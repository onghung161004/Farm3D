# Farm Restoration — Scoring Demo Expansion

## Objective

Turn the existing farming vertical slice into a polished Android-ready assessment demo. The player restores a neglected farm, completes short NPC requests, experiences a visibly living world, and demonstrates coherent low-poly art, animation, VFX, audio, and stable performance.

## Demo story

The player inherits an abandoned farm beside a small village. The village gardener asks the player to revive one field; the merchant then requests a harvested crop. Completing the two requests restores a decorative village area, providing a clear beginning, activity loop, and ending for assessment.

## Playable scope

- One compact farm-village scene built from low-poly primitives and a small, consistent palette.
- Existing farming loop remains the central action: till, seed, water, grow, harvest.
- Two NPCs: Gardener (introduces/rewards farming) and Merchant (requests one harvested crop).
- Three sequential objectives: speak to Gardener; harvest one crop; deliver it to Merchant.
- Day/night cycle of about four minutes, with directional-light color/intensity changes and a readable UI time label.
- Lightweight weather: clear and rain states; rain adds particles, sound, and a visual overlay. Rain may water one plot only if that behavior is clearly communicated.
- Feedback: tool swing, crop/harvest particles, rain, simple footsteps/interaction/harvest effects, looped ambient audio, and distinct cue sounds.
- Menu: start/pause/restart and in-game objective display.

## Art direction

Warm low-poly stylized farm: green grass, brown soil, warm wood, yellow harvest highlights, and blue rain. The player tool, objective markers, NPCs, and farm plot must contrast clearly with the terrain. Avoid downloaded art and photorealistic shaders; use URP Lit materials, primitives, baked/static lighting where possible, and limited particles.

## Performance / Android

- Keep scene below 250 active objects during ordinary gameplay.
- Use a single directional light, low-poly primitives, no real-time shadows beyond the main light where possible, and a capped particle count.
- Landscape orientation, fullscreen, `FarmDemo` first in build settings.
- Validate editor and Android smoke-test path without Console errors or crashes.

## Acceptance demo flow

1. Start at the farm; UI explains the first objective.
2. Speak to Gardener; objective changes to harvest a crop.
3. Farm one crop, with tool visual, VFX/audio, and a growth/weather/day-night cue visible.
4. Deliver the crop to Merchant; village decoration changes and completion panel appears.
5. Pause/restart works; scene stays smooth and landscape/fullscreen ready for Android.

## Explicit non-goals

- Open world, save system, animal simulation, shops/currency economy, combat, multiplayer, multiple maps, voice acting, and production-quality character models.
